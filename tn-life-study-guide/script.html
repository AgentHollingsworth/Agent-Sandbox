READHow to Read This Document — the color system▼
learn once

Four colors. Learn them once and you never have to think about it again.

🗣SAY — BLUEAnything coming out of your mouth. Statements and questions both. If it is blue, you say it.
⏸PAUSE — BLACKStop talking. Let them answer.
( )BLACK TEXT INSIDE A BLUE LINEA stage direction or a choice you make, not a word you say. If it is inside a blue line but printed black, do not read it.
↳IF… — BLACK BOLDA branch marker. Structural — tells you which path you are on.
⚠DO NOT READ ALOUD — REDYour notes. The client never hears this. Red because it is the one rule you cannot break.
🧠WHY — YELLOW HIGHLIGHTStudy note. Also never read aloud, highlighted because it is temporary. Clear the highlight once you no longer need it.

Forks are side by side. Any time the call can go two ways, both paths sit next to each other so you never have to scroll past one to find the other. Read the box, pick your column, keep moving down the page.

Why the study notes are highlighted: they are training wheels. Once they stop teaching you anything they can go, and this turns back into a clean working script without you having to keep a second version.

⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUD — the 3 rules that apply to the whole call: 1. Slow and low tone. Never rush, never pitch-voice.
2. Never read anything marked internal / red.
3. Write everything down on paper, EVERY presentation. Every single time.

10 stages
Before you memorize words, memorize the 10 stages. If you know the order, you can survive any call.

STAGE 1The Open▼
phase · OPEN
🧠 WHYYou are not selling yet. You're just confirming they're the right person and reminding them THEY asked for this. Sounding slightly unsure and friendly makes you sound human, not like a call center.
🗣 SAY:"Hi [Name]?"
⏸ PAUSE— wait for their response.
🗣 SAY:"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]…" (slight pause, sound a bit unsure but friendly) "…I wanted to quickly touch base with you about the request you made for the Final Expense Life Insurance."
🗣 SAY:"Was that insurance going to be for you or another loved one?"

Stage 1 branch — have they already been contacted?

✅ IF THEY HAVE BEEN CONTACTED
🗣 SAY:"That's awesome! Do you happen to remember which one of my agents you spoke with?"
⏸ PAUSE— let them answer.
🗣 SAY:"Just to make sure we're on the same page here — it does show as incomplete on my end."
🗣 SAY:"Which carrier did you end up getting coverage through?"
🗣 SAY:"Did they send your policy packet out to you in the mail yet?"
🚫 IF THEY HAVEN'T BEEN CONTACTED
🗣 SAY:"Got it — no problem."
🗣 SAY:"This'll only take a few minutes to run through everything with you just so you have all the information on your end."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDBoth paths land in the same place. Go to Stage 2.
STAGE 2Positioning — transition to qualification▼
phase · OPEN
🧠 WHYThis does three jobs at once: it makes you the authority ("manager"), it makes the call feel short and easy, and it flips the frame — you're not trying to sell them, you're checking if they even qualify.
🗣 SAY:"Great, I'm actually the manager here in the office assigned to your request."
🗣 SAY:"It's a pretty simple and straightforward process with me — I'll just run through 4-5 quick questions to point us in the right direction. And the biggest thing I can do throughout this process is just make sure you do actually qualify for the coverage, okay?"
STAGE 3Credentials Text — trust builder▼
phase · OPEN
🧠 WHYSending your license and business card early kills the "is this a scam?" objection before it ever gets asked. You will also reference this text again later during the application.
🗣 SAY:"First, I'm going to send you a quick text with all of my credentials — including my state license and business card — so you have everything for your records."
⏸ PAUSEbriefly. Most people will respond with "okay."
🛑 OBJECTION YOU MAY GET RIGHT HERE — "Who do you work for?"
🧠 CORE IDEAIndependent broker, name-drop the A-rated carriers, then pivot straight to pen and paper.
🗣 SAY:"We are private brokers and work exclusively with [specific lead type] on the private carrier side of things with companies like Mutual of Omaha, Americo, John Hancock, etc., and are able to show you the most affordable and best options for your needs based on your situation. It only takes me a minute or two just to get the information out to you. Do you happen to have a pen and paper you might be able to write down some of my information?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDThis answer ends on a question, which hands control back to you. Do not pause after "private brokers" and wait for approval.

biggest stage

🧠 WHYQuestions 1-5 are health/underwriting (do they qualify, and at what price). Question 6 is motivation (what they actually care about). Question 7 is existing coverage (what you're competing against).

🗣 SAY:"Okay, thank you."

🗣 SAY:"Thank you sir/ma'am."

Question 3 — Major health history

🗣 SAY:"Have you ever had a heart attack, stroke, cancer, or any kidney or liver disease?"
🗣 SAY:"Any major surgeries in the past few years?"

🗣 SAY:"Okay, thank you."

Question 6 — Their goal (this is the important one)

🗣 SAY:"So [Client's Name], when you originally filled this out, what was your main goal?"

STAGE 5Term vs. Permanent▼
conditional
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUD — USE THIS SECTION ONLY IF:they want to leave money behind AND they are UNDER 65. Otherwise skip straight to Stage 6.
🗣 SAY:"So there are typically two types of insurance we can qualify for, term or permanent."
TERM
🗣 SAY:"Term is exactly what it sounds like — it has a set period attached to it, generally around 15 years. Now once that term ends, you are left with NO coverage."
PERMANENT
🗣 SAY:"Now, permanent coverage lasts for the rest of your life, so no matter how old or sick you get, the price is locked in forever."
🗣 SAY:"So hearing those two options [Client's Name], which of those options sounds more like it suits your needs best?"
⏸ PAUSE— roll with whatever they choose.

phase · SHOW

STAGE 7Presenting the Options — Gold / Silver / Bronze▼
memorize cold

7a. Set up the medals + living benefits

🗣 SAY:"First off, I am going to have you write down GOLD, SILVER, and BRONZE just like the olympic medals. Now all of the options I'm going to share with you today come with living benefits. Are you familiar with what that means?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDMost people say no. If they say YES, read the explanation anyway.
🗣 SAY:"Living benefits basically mean that if you become terminally ill, critically injured, or chronically ill, you can access the entire face amount of your policy while you're still alive. You can use that money however you want — whether it's for medical expenses, living expenses, or anything else you might need during a period of time like that, okay?"

7b. 🥇 GOLD

🗣 SAY:"Now the GOLD option here is generally the most coverage the carriers would recommend to start out with. While this isn't the option I typically recommend, I am required to show you all of your options."
🗣 SAY:"The Gold option would be: [$30,000 if Final Expense / $100,000 if IUL or Term]. That option would be $xxx per month."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDGold typically lands between $200 – $250/month.

7c. 🥈 SILVER

🗣 SAY:"The SILVER option is what most people tend to choose as it offers a solid balance of coverage and affordability."
🗣 SAY:"The Silver option would be: [$20,000 if Final Expense / $75,000 if IUL or Term]. That option would be $xxx per month."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDSilver typically lands between $125 – $199/month.

7d. 🥉 BRONZE

🗣 SAY:"The BRONZE option is more of a 'foot-in-the-door' choice. This locks in your age, health, and habits now — while you're still as young and healthy as you'll ever be."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUD — ONLY read the next line if Bronze is genuinely the cheapest option:
🗣 SAY:"This is also the most affordable option."
🗣 SAY:"The Bronze option would be: [$15,000 if Final Expense / $50,000 if IUL or Term]. That option would be $xxx per month."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDBronze typically lands between $60 – $124/month.

STAGE 8Pick an Option — next steps▼
the hinge
🧠 WHYYou never "ask them to buy." You ask which one they'd want to try to qualify for. That's a much smaller yes.
🗣 SAY:"So [Client's Name], like I mentioned earlier, the biggest thing we can do today is just make sure this is something you do actually qualify for. If you don't, I'll have to do some homework on my end to see what other avenues we do have available for you. So if you could qualify, which option would you want to try and qualify for sir/ma'am?"
⏸ PAUSE— let them answer.
✅ IF THEY NAME AN OPTION
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDDon't oversell it. Confirm the choice and go straight to the bridge below.
🤔 IF THEY CAN'T DECIDE
🗣 SAY:"Hey [Client's Name], are you open to any suggestions?"
⏸ PAUSE— they will say yes.
🗣 SAY:"For most folks, I always recommend the Silver or Bronze option. The reason being is that we can always add coverage later but we can't decrease coverage. The last thing I want to do is take food off the table here."
🗣 SAY:"So, out of those, which would be most comfortable for you not just now but 2, 5, 10 years down the line?"

Bridge into the application

🗣 SAY:"Perfect, it'll only take me a couple more minutes here. The good news is:"
  • "We will know immediately if you do qualify."
  • "There are no medical exams or doctor visits required."
  • "Once I run through a few quick questions, you'll either get the greenlight — good to go — or they'll let us know if we need to explore other options."
🛑 OBJECTION YOU MAY GET RIGHT HERE — "I want to think about it"
🧠 WHY IT SHOWS UP HEREIt almost always lands right after you give the quotes. In their head it is about price, which means value was not built well enough. You have to take it away and rebuild.
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDThese are an escalation ladder, not five options. Work down only as far as you need to.

1. The company has to think about you too

🗣 SAY:"I completely understand, and you definitely should. However, the insurance company still has to think about you as well. So what I recommend is we just submit an application to see if you could even get approved — because if you can't, then there is nothing to even think about. If you do get approved, the insurance company will mail everything out for you to review and look over the details. Okay?"

2. No commitment today

🗣 SAY:"The cool thing is, no final decisions or commitments are made today. What we do is submit an application to see if you qualify. Worst case, you don't qualify and we look at some other options. Best case, you do qualify, the company mails you out a physical policy and gives you 30 days to review all the fine print numbers to make sure this is going to benefit you and your family in the event of a death or disability."

3. The shopping-around rebuild

🗣 SAY:"Yeah, I hear you there. Honestly, I'm the same way — always shopping for the cheapest prices so I don't get ripped off, especially with a product that I'm not very familiar with. Now, like I mentioned before, I do the medical underwriting for every A to A+ rated carrier in the state of [_____], so I was able to plug in the health conditions you made me aware of, and after hearing about your situation and the kind of coverage that would benefit you and your family the most, this is what I was able to come up with. Now unfortunately, before you can buy and pay for anything, we need to make sure you're able to medically qualify — so that's what we are going to knock out today. I've got the application already filled out for the most part, I just have to confirm a couple more things…"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDAsk the first question on the app, then circle back to picking an option right before the Social question.

4. If they are STILL hesitant (concerned tone)

🗣 SAY:"I guess a better question is, what were you hoping to hear from another insurance company…?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDStay quiet. They will reveal their main concern.

5. The closer

🗣 SAY:"Yeah, I completely understand and I want you to think about it. However, these are just numbers on paper right now. The best thing we can do is see if you medically qualify, so that when you are thinking about it, you know the prices we're talking about are real."

9. Simplified issue explanation + Social (Insured page)

🧠 WHYThis whole paragraph exists to make the SSN request feel routine. Say it smoothly and the Social usually comes without a fight.
🗣 SAY:"Now, everything is simplified issued. They won't send a doctor out to prick your finger, draw blood, or make you pee in a cup. Ever since COVID, all the insurance carriers switched over to simplified issued. All they will do is just verify your Social through the MIB, which is the Medical Information Bureau, just to do a quick prescription check to confirm you're healthy enough for the coverage. The main things they're checking for are that you don't have:"
  • "No stage 4 cancer"
  • "Not confined to a hospital bed 24 hours a day"
  • "They mainly want to make sure you're just not on your final days trying to open up a life insurance policy, okay?"
🗣 SAY:"And I'm assuming you have your social memorized?"
✅ IF THEY GIVE IT
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDTake the number, stay casual, keep moving. Do not thank them twice or it starts to feel like a big deal.
❌ IF THEY REFUSE
🗣 SAY:"Just to clarify, they only need it to verify your prescription check through the MIB to make sure that you are healthy for the coverage, and to also verify if you are actually applying for the insurance in your name — it's a standard part of the application process."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDIf they push back again, use the objection below.
🛑 OBJECTION RIGHT HERE — "Why do you need my Social?"
🧠 CORE IDEAI don't need it. The carrier does, for the MIB health check. Pick whichever version sounds more natural coming out of your mouth.
VERSION A — the due diligence angle
🗣 SAY:"So I don't need your Social, the insurance company does, to be able to do their due diligence. If they are going to provide you with thousands of dollars in coverage that will go to your family one day, they need to make sure you are healthy enough for the coverage. How they do that is they verify your Social through the Medical Information Bureau to make sure you don't have any critical or chronic illness. Does that make sense? Okay, I'm ready when you are."
VERSION B — the no-needles angle
🗣 SAY:"Absolutely, I understand your concerns. That's why I sent you all my information for your records. This is a simplified issue life insurance policy, so what that means is no nurse needs to come out to draw blood or take urine, and all they do is check the MIB — the Medical Information Bureau — to see if you are healthy enough to qualify for the coverage. Does that make sense? Okay, I'm ready when you are."

9b — Americo authorization code

🗣 SAY:"Alright, you're going to receive a 6-digit code via text message from Americo Life Insurance, sent from an 877 number."
🗣 SAY:"All I need you to do is read back the 6-digit code from the message. Ignore the link in the text — you don't need to click it."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDThe message will read: "Thank you for applying with Americo Life Insurance. Your 6-digit authorization code is [CODE]."
🗣 SAY:"Please read the code back to me when you receive it."

Once you receive the code

🗣 SAY:"Perfect! From here, I'll click through some questions you've already answered. Based on those, you'll either receive a preliminary green light, which means you're about 95% approved, or you'll be booted out, and we'll need to go back to the drawing board together to explore other options you might qualify for."
🗣 SAY:"Give me just a couple of moments while I get some answers."

9c — Green light: fill out the application

⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDFill out the application at this point.
🗣 SAY:"Now [Client's Name], we are getting all of those initial preliminary green lights back, so we are looking as good as we can up until this point — so congratulations."
🗣 SAY:"Now, who would be playing the role of your beneficiary on something like this?"
🗣 SAY:"Okay, great. Can you confirm their relationship to you?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDGet the beneficiary's FULL NAME and PHONE NUMBER. Write it on paper AND put it in the app.

9d — 💳 Banking information

1. Set the payment date

🗣 SAY:"Now [Client's Name], is there a specific day of the month you'd like this to regularly take effect?"
⏸ PAUSE— let them give you a date.
🗣 SAY:"Perfect! So I will have this policy go into effect on [selected date], and that will reoccur on the [selected date] of each calendar month, okay sir/ma'am."

2. Confirm the bank

🗣 SAY:"Just to confirm, Americo does partner with your financial institution. Who do you typically bank with?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDOnce they name the bank, quickly Google the routing number with their state, e.g. "Chase routing number Arizona."
🗣 SAY:"Awesome! It does look like [CARRIER] partners with [Bank Name]."

3. Verify the routing number

🗣 SAY:"If I read back the routing number that the carrier has listed here, would you be able to recognize it, or do you need a moment to pull it up?"
✅ IF THEY CONFIRM
🗣 SAY:"Great! The routing number the carrier has here is: [123456789]"
❓ IF THEY'RE UNSURE
🗣 SAY:"No worries! Do you have your bank's mobile app or a check book laying around?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDGuide them to find the routing number in the app.
🗣 SAY:"Once you have it pulled up, let me know."

4. Confirm the account number

🗣 SAY:"Perfect! Now, what account number would you like on file?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDListen carefully, then read it back.
🗣 SAY:"Just to double-check, I have [read back the account number]. Does that sound correct?"
🛑 OBJECTION RIGHT HERE — "I don't want to give you my banking info"
🧠 CORE IDEAThey are verifying identity, not taking money today. Say the words "no payments are due today" out loud, because that is the actual fear.

First pass:

🗣 SAY:"The biggest thing the insurance carrier is doing is making sure it is actually you applying for insurance in your name. For example, I couldn't apply for this coverage in your name with my bank account — that would be insurance fraud. Does that make sense? No payments are due today or coming out; they are just doing their due diligence to make sure everything is as it should be on their end."

If they say no again:

🗣 SAY:"Again, the only reason they are asking for this is to verify it is you. Ever since the 2001 PATRIOT Act, they require insurance to be tied to the person's correct account because of all the insurance fraud that happened during 9/11."

9e — After banking: the final code

🗣 SAY:"Okay, perfect! I'm filling out a couple pieces of my agent information, then you will get one last 6-digit code — just like the first one."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDSEND CODE.
🗣 SAY:"Now from here, they will do their final underwriting check. A couple things can happen here: we can either get instantly approved for the coverage, or they can take it to further underwriting, which just means the insurance carrier is crossing their T's and dotting their I's — so bear with me just a moment here [Client's Name]."

9f — The two outcomes

🧠 WHYThese are the only two ways this call ends. Read them side by side until you can deliver either one without a pause, because the client will hear any hesitation as bad news.
✅ IF THEY DID QUALIFY (the punchline)
🗣 SAY:"Alright [Client's Name], do you want the good news or the bad news first?"
🗣 SAY:"Bad news is you're stuck with me forever — because the good news is you did get instantly approved."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDLaugh a little bit. Go to Stage 10.
⏳ IF IT GOES TO FURTHER UNDERWRITING
🗣 SAY:"Alright [Client's Name], after submitting everything, it looks like the application is going through further underwriting. No worries at all — this is completely normal, as about 85% of policies require this step. It simply means the insurance carrier is doing their due diligence, crossing their T's and dotting their I's. We should have a final decision within the next 24-48 hours."
🗣 SAY:"In the meantime, I'll give the underwriters a call as soon as we're off the phone to see if I can expedite any answers on my end. Would you be available in the next 15-20 minutes if I give them a call to get some answers?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDSet the callback time, then go to Stage 10.
STAGE 10Cement the Sale — once approved▼
do not skip
🧠 WHYThis section is what stops cancellations and makes the claim actually get paid. Do not skip it because you're excited about the sale.

Receiving the packet

🗣 SAY:"You will receive the physical documents within the next 7-10 business days. Once you get the packet, if you have any questions please feel free to give me a call. We can address any questions you may have or make any necessary changes if needed."

Put your name on the packet

🗣 SAY:"When your policy packet arrives, I want you to grab a sharpie or a dark pen and write my name and number right on the front of that packet."
🗣 SAY:"God forbid something happened to you, I want your family to know exactly who to call — not to be flipping through papers trying to figure out what to do, okay? I'll be the person your beneficiary reaches out to, and I'll personally walk them through everything step by step."
🗣 SAY:"Now obviously, it's important that you let [beneficiary's name] know they're listed. Because if something did happen, they'll be the ones handling this. Make sure they're aware, and I promise I'll do my part and make sure they understand exactly how this works and what steps to take, if you would like."
🗣 SAY:"Any questions on your end while you have me [Client's Name]?"

Effective date — pick ONE

↳ IF PAYMENT IS PROCESSING TODAY
🗣 SAY:"Lastly, this is a first day coverage policy, so even before that policy packet gets out to you, the moment that payment comes out you're all set."
↳ IF THEY CHOSE A LATER DATE
🗣 SAY:"Lastly, as a reminder, this coverage won't take effect until [EFFECTIVE DATE], so no skydiving or drag racing until after that date, okay sir?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDLaugh a little bit.

Your personal contact

🗣 SAY:"Please save this number — it's my personal line, the same one my family calls me on. I am available 7 days a week by call or text. If you need anything, don't hesitate to reach out."
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDSay your goodbyes.

optional
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDDo not open this section until you are fluent through Stage 10. Learn the sale first.
🧠 WHY (study note)This block follows a pattern. Learn the four letters and the whole thing is easy to remember:
  • V = Value check ("1 to 10, how did I do?")
  • I = Impact ("what was most beneficial?")
  • B = Beneficiaries (offer to walk them through it)
  • S = Someone else ("who else would thank you?")

(V) Value check

🗣 SAY:"Ok, now the last thing I always ask my families before I let you go here today — on a scale of 1 to 10, how did I do for you today?"
⏸ PAUSE— wait for their answer.
🗣 SAY:"Awesome, thank you so much, it's been a pleasure to protect your family."

(I) Impact

🗣 SAY:"Now out of everything we covered, what did you feel was most beneficial about what we went over?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDRepeat it back to them and expand on it.

(B) Beneficiaries

🗣 SAY:"Now obviously, like I mentioned before, I will make sure we go over your policy with your beneficiaries if needed so they know exactly how that works, God forbid something happens to you. In addition, I'll make sure they get access to the same exact information as we went over with you. Now, do you want me to go over your full policy with them, or just the name of the company and the policy number?"

(S) Someone else

🗣 SAY:"Ok, great. Other than your beneficiaries, I know there are definitely some people in your life who can benefit from this — let's go ahead and start with [whoever they mentioned in the presentation: brother, sister, parents, aunts, uncles, coworkers, best friends, etc.]…"
🗣 SAY:"What was your sister's name again? Ok great, and does she have a [***] area code like you? Perfect. Now does your sister work a day shift, nights, or not working? Kids, married?"
🗣 SAY:"Anyone else you care about that would thank you for putting them in a better spot if something happened?"
⚠ DO NOT READ ALOUDPrompt softly — "Who's next, who's next…" — until they say they're out twice.

Recap + beat the referral rebuttals before they happen

🗣 SAY:"Perfect. So here's the game plan — I'll give them a quick call or text, just like I did with you. All I ask is that you give them a heads-up. Just say: 'Hey, I talked with [YOUR NAME], they helped me get my life insurance taken care of and they're going to reach out to you — it's actually really good info.'"
🗣 SAY:"Can you do that for me?"
⏸ PAUSE— get verbal confirmation.
🗣 SAY:"Awesome. And hey — if they're all set, no big deal. At the very least, I'll make sure they're in the best possible spot. And if they do move forward, they'll owe you dinner, or at least a bottle of wine!"
🗣 SAY:"Alright [Client's Name], please follow up with me as soon as that packet arrives if you have questions, and I appreciate you thinking of the people you care about. That's what this is really about."
🗣 SAY:"Thanks again — and have a blessed day!"

summaries
🧠These are learning tools, not script lines. Nothing here is new material — it's a summary of what's above.

The 7 qualifying questions, in order

1Date of birth
2Medications (+ insulin/pills, A1C, past prescriptions)
3Heart attack, stroke, cancer, kidney/liver + major surgeries
4Smoke?
5Height + weight
6What was your main goal?
7Do you currently have coverage? (private / employer / VA)

Everything you must write on paper

  • Date of birth
  • Medications and conditions
  • Height and weight
  • Smoker yes/no
  • Their stated goal
  • Existing coverage: carrier, amount, monthly premium
  • State of birth
  • Mailing address, email, phone
  • Chosen option (Gold/Silver/Bronze)
  • Beneficiary FULL NAME, relationship, PHONE NUMBER
  • Bank name, routing number, account number, draft date

Phrases to say exactly as written (highest leverage lines)

  • "The biggest thing I can do throughout this process is just make sure you do actually qualify."
  • "We can always add coverage later but we can't decrease coverage."
  • "Which option would you want to try and qualify for?"
  • "No final decisions or commitments are made today."
  • "These are just numbers on paper right now."

The 5 most common places new agents lose the call

1Talking fast — the whole script dies without slow and low tone.
2Reading internal/red notes out loud.
3Presenting prices before they have a pen and paper.
4Skipping the credentials text, then fighting the "is this a scam" objection later.
5Skipping Stage 10 (cement the sale) and Stage 11 (referrals) because the sale already closed.
