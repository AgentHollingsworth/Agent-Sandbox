What to get out of each one while you're still licensing

🧠 WHY THIS MATTERS
  • Monday — Boot Camp. Built for exactly where you are. Never miss it.
  • Tuesday — Chase. Rotating topics. Take notes even on things that don't apply yet.
  • Wednesday — Sales & rebuttals. Listen only. You haven't learned the script yet and this call goes deep. Don't try to keep up and don't feel behind — you're here for the tone and the culture. It'll make a lot more sense after Day 8.
  • Thursday — Agency / housekeeping. This is how the business actually runs. Worth understanding early if you ever want your own team.
  • Friday — Execute weekly. The big one. Org-wide numbers, who's winning, what's working. Never miss it.
  • Saturday — Separation Saturday. Optional while you're studying for the exam — your course comes first on weekends. Expected once you're producing.
WATCH OUTShow up even when you have nothing to report. Especially then. During licensing this call is the only thing tying you to the team, and agents who start skipping it in week one are usually gone by week four. It's an hour. Protect it.
DO THISGet the daily call link from your manager and put 8:00 AM Arizona on your calendar as recurring. You're on it starting tomorrow — or today if you started on a call day. The full 14-day plan lives in the Training Checklist.
